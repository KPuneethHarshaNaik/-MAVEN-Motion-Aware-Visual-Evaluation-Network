Pipeline

How It Works

From raw video footage to clinical-grade prediction in four stages.

▶
Input Video
Raw footage of a child in a natural environment. 16 key frames are sampled.
⊞
MobileNetV3-Small
Extracts spatial features from each frame — body pose, gaze, and motion cues.
◇
Transformer Encoder
Models temporal dependencies across all 16 frames using multi-head self-attention.
◎
MLP Output
Binary classification head outputs ASD or Non-ASD with calibrated confidence.
